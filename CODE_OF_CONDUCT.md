# Code of Conduct

We welcome thoughtful, respectful contributors. Be patient, inclusive and constructive; focus critiques on ideas and implementations rather than personal characteristics.

Harassment, threats, hateful statements, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, publishing confidential information, and repeated disruption are not welcome. Maintainers may moderate, warn or restrict participation.

Use GitHub's report features to flag abuse. Do not publicly expose confidential incident reports. Decisions involving a reported maintainer should be reviewed by someone not implicated when possible.

See the [organization-wide policy](https://github.com/primordialweave/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) for the broader community expectations.
